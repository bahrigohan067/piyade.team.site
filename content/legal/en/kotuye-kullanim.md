# Abuse and Violation Reporting Procedure

To keep **Piyade World (Piyade Dünyası)** safe, respectful, and compliant with applicable law, anyone may report safety, rule, or legal violations through a transparent review process.

## 1. What Can Be Reported?

Through the [Report Abuse](/en/bildir) portal (no login required), you can report across 10 categories:

| Category | Reviewing Team | Priority / Target Initial Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🆘 Child Safety | Community Official (Moderation) | **Critical** — Urgent priority (target < 4 hours) |
| ⚠️ Threat / Violence | Community Official (Moderation) | **High** — Target < 12 hours |
| 🔞 Sexual / Inappropriate Content | Community Official (Moderation) | **High** — Target < 24 hours |
| 🚨 Harassment / Bullying | Community Official (Moderation) | **Normal** — Target 24–48 hours |
| 🎭 Scam / Spam | Community Official (Moderation) | **Normal** — Target 24–48 hours |
| 🪪 Account / Identity Impersonation | Community Official (Moderation) | **Normal** — Target 24–48 hours |
| ⚖️ Illegal Content | Management (Developer & Directors) | **High** — Target < 24 hours |
| 🔐 Personal Data / Privacy Violation (KVKK) | Management (Developer & Directors) | **High** — Target < 24 hours |
| ©️ Copyright / IP (DMCA) | Management (Developer & Directors) | **Normal** — Target 24–72 hours |
| 📌 Other Site / Safety Concerns | Management (Developer & Directors) | **Low / Normal** — Target 48–72 hours |

> **In-Chat Messages:** To report a specific private chat message, please use the **"Report"** button directly on that message so the surrounding 30-message snapshot is captured automatically. The [Report Abuse](/en/bildir) page complements — and does not replace — in-chat reporting.

## 2. How to Submit and Track a Report

1. Pick a category from the keypad on [Report Abuse](/en/bildir).
2. Choose the incident location (*Site, Chat, Discord, Other*), provide a URL/ID if available, and describe the issue (up to 2,000 characters).
3. Optionally attach up to **3 evidence screenshots** (`PNG`, `JPG`, `WEBP`, up to **5 MB** each). Evidence files are stored privately behind access controls and shown blurred by default to reviewers.
4. Save your **`RPT-YYYY-XXXXX`** tracking code and check status updates (*New, In Review, Action Taken, No Basis, Closed*) on [Report Status Lookup](/en/bildir/sorgula).

## 3. Impartiality, Conflict of Interest, and Audit Trail

- Staff members involved in or submitting a report cannot access that record (**conflict-of-interest block**).
- Reviewer permissions are verified **fresh** against the Bot API on every request.
- Every view and status change is recorded in a contentless audit log.
- Report records and evidence files are retained for up to **{{REPORT_RETENTION_DAYS}} days**.

## 4. Possible Outcomes

Depending on findings, actions may include content removal, verbal or official disciplinary warning ([General Rules](/en/kurallar)), chat mute/ban, membership revocation, or escalation to authorities where legally required. Unfounded reports are closed with an internal justification note.

## 5. False Reports & Appeals

- Deliberately abusive, retaliatory, or fabricated reports may result in disciplinary sanctions.
- To appeal an enforcement outcome or report resolution, contact `{{LEGAL_EMAIL}}` with your tracking code or reach out to senior management on our Discord server.
