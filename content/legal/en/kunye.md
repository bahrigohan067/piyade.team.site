## 2. Hosting Provider Responsibility

- Piyade World provides a community portal where players register game profiles, view community statistics, and use authorized private messaging.
- Under Article 5 of Law No. 5651, hosting providers are not obligated to proactively monitor user content for illegality, but act promptly to remove or disable access to unlawful content upon proper notification.
