# KVKK Data Subject Request Form & Procedure

To exercise your personal data rights under Articles 11 and 13 of Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you may submit the online form on this page or use any of the alternative official channels below.

## 1. Before You Submit

- **Response Deadline:** Applications are answered as promptly as possible and **within {{KVKK_RESPONSE_DEADLINE_DAYS}} days at the latest**.
- **Fee Policy:** Requests are handled free of charge unless processing incurs an additional statutory cost set by the Personal Data Protection Board.
- **Identity Verification:** To prevent unauthorized disclosure of personal data, we may request reasonable additional information to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.
- **Processing of Application Data:** Information entered in this form is processed solely to evaluate, respond to, and document compliance with your KVKK request and is stored in an access-controlled area for **{{KVKK_RETENTION_DAYS}} days** (~{{KVKK_RETENTION_YEARS}} years).

## 2. Tracking Code & Status Lookup

After submission, you receive a unique **tracking code** (`KVKK-YYYY-XXXXX`). Using your tracking code and email address on the [Status Lookup page](/en/yasal/kvkk-basvuru/sorgula), you can check your application status (*Received*, *In Review*, *Answered*, *Rejected*). Personal request details are never displayed on the status lookup page.
