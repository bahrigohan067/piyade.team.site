# Intellectual Property & Copyright Policy (Law No. 5846 & DMCA)

**Piyade World (Piyade Dünyası)** respects intellectual property rights. We review and act promptly on valid copyright infringement notices in accordance with Turkish Law No. 5846 on Intellectual and Artistic Works (FSEK) and standard **DMCA** notice-and-takedown procedures.

## 1. How to Submit a Copyright / DMCA Notice

If you believe content on our site infringes your copyright, you may submit a notice **without creating an account or logging in**:

- **Online Form (Recommended):** Open [Report Abuse](/en/bildir) and choose **"Copyright / IP (DMCA)"**.
- **Email:** `{{LEGAL_EMAIL}}` (include `Copyright / DMCA Notice` in the subject line).
- **Postal / KEP:** `{{LEGAL_ADDRESS}}` / `{{LEGAL_KEP}}` (Attn: {{LEGAL_CONTROLLER_NAME}}).

## 2. Required Information in a Valid Notice

1. **Rights Holder or Authorized Agent:** Full name/organization and a valid contact email.
2. **Description of the Copyrighted Work:** Clear identification of the original work and reference link if available.
3. **Location of Infringing Material:** Exact URL(s) or content/account ID on our site.
4. **Good-Faith Belief Statement:** Statement that you have a good-faith belief the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. **Accuracy Statement:** Statement that the information provided is accurate and you are the rights holder or authorized to act on their behalf.

## 3. Review & Takedown Process

- Valid notices receive a tracking code (`RPT-YYYY-XXXXX`) and route directly to the **Management Team** (`/yonetim/bildirimler`).
- Initial review typically occurs within **24–72 hours**. Verified infringing material is removed or access-disabled promptly.
- Accounts engaged in repeat infringement may be restricted or terminated.

## 4. Counter-Notice Procedure

If you believe your content was removed by mistake or misidentification, send a counter-notice citing the tracking code to `{{LEGAL_EMAIL}}` or via [Report Abuse](/en/bildir), including your contact details, the prior location of the content, and your good-faith explanation of the error.

## 5. Misrepresentation Warning

Submitting knowingly false or bad-faith copyright claims may result in legal liability. Please verify whether fair use, quotation, or an open license applies before submitting a claim.
